# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Stack

Electron (Web Audio, Canvas 2D), same as apps/nebula. Windows + Linux, least code.

## Users

Someone who puts it on a screen to unsettle themselves or a room: art piece, party loop, stream background.

## Product Purpose

Split the screen into many clocks that all disagree. Some run fast or slow, some are offset by seconds, some count down, one analogue face is mirrored and upside down. Clocks occasionally stop, reverse or glitch. Ticks, chimes and a heartbeat play now and then. Panels breathe brighter and darker in waves. The goal is beautiful, convincing disorientation.

## Operating Context

Fullscreen, cursor hidden, no chrome. Runs unattended for long stretches.

## Product Principles

- Every clock must look trustworthy on its own; the chaos comes from them disagreeing.
- Never still: something is always drifting, breathing or misbehaving.
- Sound is sparse and real-sounding, so each tick or heartbeat lands.
